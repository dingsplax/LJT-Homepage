**Authors:** Junteng Liu, Weihao Zeng, Xiwen Zhang, Yijun Wang, Zifei Shan, Junxian He

**Abstract:** We identify and analyze the perception bottleneck in vision-language models (VLMs) for chart understanding tasks. Our findings reveal fundamental limitations in how VLMs process and understand visual chart data, and we propose methods to mitigate these bottlenecks.
