**Authors:** Yuzhen Huang, Yuzhuo Bai, Zhihao Zhu, Junlei Zhang, Jinghan Zhang, Tangjun Su, Junteng Liu, Chuancheng Lv, Yikai Zhang, Jiayi Lei, Yao Fu, Maosong Sun, Junxian He

**Abstract:** We present C-Eval, a comprehensive Chinese evaluation suite for foundation models. C-Eval covers 52 diverse disciplines, spanning varying levels of difficulty, providing a rigorous and holistic benchmark for assessing the capabilities of foundation models in Chinese.
