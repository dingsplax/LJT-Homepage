**Authors:** Junteng Liu, Shiqi Chen, Yu Cheng, Junxian He

**Abstract:** We discover that large language models contain a universal truthfulness hyperplane in their internal representation space. This hyperplane can be used to detect truthful vs. false statements with high accuracy, and we demonstrate how interventions along this direction can improve model truthfulness.
