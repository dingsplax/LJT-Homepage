**Authors:** Junteng Liu, Yuanxiang Fan, Zhuo Jiang, Han Ding, Yongyi Hu, Chi Zhang, Yiqi Shi, Shitong Weng, Aili Chen, Shiqi Chen, Yunan Huang, Mozhi Zhang, Pengyu Zhao, Junjie Yan, Junxian He

**Abstract:** We introduce SynLogic, a scalable framework for synthesizing verifiable reasoning data that significantly improves logical reasoning in large language models. Our approach generates high-quality reasoning traces with automated verification, enabling more robust and reliable logical deduction capabilities.
