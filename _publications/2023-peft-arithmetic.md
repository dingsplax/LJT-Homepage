**Authors:** Jinghan Zhang, Shiqi Chen, Junteng Liu, Junxian He

**Abstract:** We show that parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT) modules can be effectively composed using simple arithmetic operations such as addition and subtraction. This allows us to combine different task adaptations and create new model capabilities without additional training, significantly improving the flexibility and reusability of PEFT methods.
