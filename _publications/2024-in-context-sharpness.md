**Authors:** Shiqi Chen, Miao Xiong, Junteng Liu, Zhengxuan Wu, Teng Xiao, Siyang Gao, Junxian He

**Abstract:** We introduce in-context sharpness as a novel signal derived from the internal representations of large language models that can serve as an early alert for hallucinated content. Our method provides an effective and efficient approach to detecting and mitigating hallucinations during generation.
